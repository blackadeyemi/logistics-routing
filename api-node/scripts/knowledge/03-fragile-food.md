# Fragile items, food and cakes

## Packaging
Fragile items must be packed by the seller in a box with padding. If the packaging looks unsafe, the rider photographs it and calls the dispatcher before accepting the pickup. Do not accept glass items that are not wrapped.

## Cakes and food
Cakes go on vans or keke, never on bikes, unless the cake box is smaller than 25 cm and the customer accepts the risk in writing. Food and cakes are delivered first on a route, within 90 minutes of pickup. Keep them out of direct sun and never stack anything on top of a cake box.

## Damaged on the way
If an item is damaged in transit, the rider takes a photo, does not hand it over, and calls the dispatcher. The company covers damage up to ₦50,000 when the packaging rules above were followed.
