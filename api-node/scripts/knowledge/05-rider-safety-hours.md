# Rider safety, hours and weather

## Working hours
Pickups run from 8am to 5pm and deliveries until 8pm, Monday to Saturday. Same-day delivery is available for orders received before 1pm. Orders after 1pm go out the next working day unless the dispatcher agrees otherwise.

## Rain and flooding
During heavy rain, riders stop and wait in a safe place; no delivery is worth an accident. Known flood points include parts of Lekki-Epe Expressway near Ajah, Oworonshoki and Apapa Road. The dispatcher may move deliveries to the next day and tells customers.

## Safety
Riders wear a helmet at all times and never carry passengers. If a rider feels unsafe at an address, they leave and call the dispatcher. Riders do not enter customers' homes.
