# Estates, offices and gated access

## Gated estates
Most estates in Lekki, Ajah and Magodo require the rider to tell security the resident's name and house number. Where a gate code or access instruction is known, it is in the dropoff notes. Never share gate codes with anyone outside the company.

## Victoria Garden City and Lekki Phase 1
VGC security asks for the resident's name and a phone call from the resident. Call the customer when you reach the main gate, not when you reach the house. In Lekki Phase 1 some streets close to traffic after 10pm.

## Offices and towers
For office towers on Victoria Island and Ikoyi, deliver to reception unless the dropoff notes say otherwise. Get the receptionist's name and write it in the app. Many towers do not allow riders above the ground floor.
