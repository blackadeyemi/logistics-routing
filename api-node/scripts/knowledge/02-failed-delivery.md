# Failed deliveries and returns

## Before marking a delivery as failed
Call the customer twice, five minutes apart, from the delivery address. Send a WhatsApp message saying you are at the address. Wait at least 15 minutes in total. Only then mark the dropoff as failed in the driver app, so the time of every attempt is recorded.

## What happens next
Failed items go back to the office the same day, not to the seller. The dispatcher contacts the seller to agree a new delivery date. A second attempt is free; a third attempt is charged at the normal delivery price.

## Returns from customers
When a customer returns an item to a seller, the pickup is at the customer and the dropoff is the seller's shop. Check the item matches the order description before leaving the customer, and note any damage in the pickup notes.
