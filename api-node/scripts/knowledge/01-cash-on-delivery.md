# Cash on delivery

## Collecting payment
Riders collect the exact amount written in the dropoff notes before handing over the item. Accept cash or a bank transfer to the company account only, never to a rider's personal account. For a transfer, wait until the dispatcher confirms the money has arrived; a screenshot from the customer is not proof of payment.

## When the customer cannot pay
If the customer cannot pay the full amount, do not hand over the item and do not accept part payment. Call the dispatcher from the doorstep. The dispatcher calls the seller, and the rider waits no more than 10 minutes for a decision. If there is no decision, mark the delivery as failed and bring the item back.

## Handing over the cash
Riders hand over all cash collected to the dispatcher at the end of the day, with the order numbers it belongs to. Amounts above ₦100,000 are handed over the same day before 6pm, even if the route is not finished.
