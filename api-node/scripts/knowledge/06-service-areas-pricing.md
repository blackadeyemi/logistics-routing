# Service areas and pricing

## Where we deliver
We deliver within Lagos State only: the Mainland (Ikeja, Yaba, Surulere, Maryland, Ogba, Gbagada, Oshodi, Ikorodu, Festac, Apapa) and the Island (Victoria Island, Ikoyi, Lekki, Ajah). We do not deliver outside Lagos, for example to Ibadan, Abeokuta or Ota; refer those customers to an interstate courier.

## Prices
Delivery prices by zone. Mainland to Mainland (for example Ikeja to Yaba or Surulere): ₦2,500. Island to Island (for example Victoria Island to Lekki or Ajah): ₦3,000. Mainland to Island or Island to Mainland (for example Ikeja to Lekki, or Ikoyi to Maryland): ₦4,000. Ikorodu, Ajah beyond Sangotedo, and Badagry add ₦1,500. Items over 20 kg go by van and are priced by the dispatcher.

## Express
Express delivery within 3 hours costs double the normal price and is only available for orders received before 2pm.
